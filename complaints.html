Step by step

How to report a late-night disturbance

Follow these steps to give your complaint the best chance of action.

  1. Note the address or map link, the time and the source (party, DJ, loudspeaker, construction, vehicle).
  2. For a live disturbance, call 112.
  3. For repeated or industrial noise, file an online complaint with the CPCB portal or your State Pollution Control Board.
  4. Keep brief evidence and save any complaint number you receive.

Under the Noise Pollution (Regulation and Control) Rules, 2000, loudspeakers and public address systems are generally restricted between 10 PM and 6 AM, with limited exceptions. Rules can vary by state.
