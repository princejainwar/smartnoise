About SmartNoise

SmartNoise is an independent project that helps people in India respond to noise pollution with clear information and simple monitoring tools.

Our mission

Make noise pollution easier to understand and act on

Urban noise is a daily problem, but the steps to address it are often hard to find. SmartNoise puts helplines, official complaint portals and reference limits in one place.

It pairs this guidance with a lightweight dashboard so anyone can see how loud their surroundings are, using only a browser.

Privacy first

No server. Accounts and readings stay in your browser.

Official sources

Limits follow the Noise Pollution (Regulation and Control) Rules, 2000.

Transparent method

Estimates are labelled as such, with an open calculation.

Free and open

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no external libraries.

Vision

Where SmartNoise is headed

Planned improvements for future versions of the platform.

IoT sensor nodes

Connect dedicated low-cost sensors for continuous, location-based monitoring.

Calibration and A-weighting

Calibrate against a reference meter and add A-weighting for closer dB(A) accuracy.

Cloud sync and maps

Optional shared accounts and map views of noise across a city.

What SmartNoise is not

SmartNoise is not a certified measurement tool and its readings cannot prove a violation of the Noise Rules. For legal matters, rely on calibrated measurements by the authorities.
