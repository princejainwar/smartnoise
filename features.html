Dashboard / Features

Everything in one noise toolkit

Guidance for residents and a monitoring dashboard for anyone who wants to measure and understand noise, all in a single free product.

01

Live noise monitor

Estimate the noise level around you in real time using your device microphone, with a clear status from low to very high.

Open Live Monitor
  • RMS-based estimate converted to dB with an adjustable calibration offset
  • Animated waveform and live trend chart
  • Status updates against the selected reference limit
  • Microphone is released as soon as you stop or leave the page
02

Demo Mode

No microphone? Launch Demo Mode to explore the full dashboard with realistic simulated values for your chosen area.

Try Demo Mode
  • Always clearly labelled as SIMULATED DATA
  • Includes occasional simulated loud events to test alerts
  • Ideal for presentations and first-time exploration
03

Reference limits and alerts

Pick an area type and time of day. SmartNoise compares readings with the CPCB / MoEFCC limits and raises an alert when they are exceeded.

View noise limits
  • Industrial, commercial, residential and silence zones
  • Separate day (6 AM to 10 PM) and night (10 PM to 6 AM) limits
  • Alert with level, area, threshold, time and recommended action
04

History, analytics and export

Readings are recorded roughly every five seconds while monitoring and stored privately in your browser.

Open Analytics
  • Summary statistics: count, average, maximum, minimum, alerts
  • Trend chart across saved readings and a searchable history table
  • One-click CSV export; delete single records or clear everything
05

Noise zones and recommendations

See typical noise by zone type in a conceptual smart-city view, and get practical recommendations for the selected area.

View Noise Zones
  • Residential, hospital, school, junction, market and industrial zones
  • Illustrative values, always labelled as demo data
  • Tailored guidance for every area type
06

Guidance and official links

Beyond the dashboard, SmartNoise brings together the numbers and portals you need when noise becomes a problem.

See helplines
  • 112, 100 and Delhi 155271 helplines
  • Links to CPCB, CPGRAMS, 112 India and the Delhi noise portal
  • Plain-language steps for reporting a late-night disturbance

SmartNoise microphone readings are prototype estimates, not calibrated dB SPL. Actual environmental noise measurement requires a calibrated sound-level meter.
